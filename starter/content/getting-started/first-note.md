## Write a note

Create a Markdown file in `content/`. Its filename becomes the page URL.

## Organize folders

Use `_index.md` to name and order a folder. Folders expand in the sidebar but do not have pages of their own.

## Customize your site

Change the title, brand, logo, language, and footer in `hugo.toml`.
